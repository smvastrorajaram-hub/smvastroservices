Traditional Marriage Porutham: Common Factors and Responsible Reading

A clear introduction to commonly used Tamil marriage-compatibility porutham factors, their traditional purpose and the limits of a matching score.

What Porutham is intended to do

Jathaga Porutham is a traditional method used in some Tamil families when discussing marriage compatibility. Many systems begin with the birth Nakshatra and Rasi of both people and compare a set of named factors. Different regional schools and family traditions may use different lists, weights and additional checks, so a report should state which method it follows.

A Porutham result is a cultural and astrological interpretation. It cannot measure the quality of a relationship, establish consent, or guarantee whether a marriage will be successful. It should not be used as the sole basis for a major personal decision.

Commonly named porutham factors

  1. Dina Porutham: a traditional star-distance check associated with general harmony and well-being.
  2. Gana Porutham: compares the Nakshatra gana classifications used in the chosen tradition.
  3. Mahendra Porutham: one of the traditional checks associated with continuity and supportive family life.
  4. Sthree Deergha: considers the relative distance between the birth stars under a customary counting rule.
  5. Yoni Porutham: compares symbolic animal classifications assigned to Nakshatras.
  6. Rasi Porutham: examines the relationship between the two Moon signs in the selected system.
  7. Rasi Athipathi: considers the relationship between the lords of the two Moon signs.
  8. Vasya Porutham: uses traditional sign-to-sign influence or attraction groupings.
  9. Rajju Porutham: a specific traditional grouping check that some schools treat as an important caution factor.
  10. Vedha Porutham: checks star pairings described as mutually obstructive in the chosen table.

Not every tradition uses exactly these ten items, and some systems apply additional chart-level checks. The spelling of a term can also vary between Tamil and English transliteration.

How to read a matching report

First confirm the birth details for both people independently. A mistaken birth star can change several star-based checks at once. Then identify the method and list of poruthams used by the report. Read each factor's explanation rather than relying only on a total score, colour or single “match / no match” label.

Where a report offers a deeper chart review, understand which additional factors are included and which are not. A score from one table should not be compared directly with a different school's score unless their rules and weights are the same. If the birth time is unknown, do not invent a precise time to make the report appear complete.

Personal decisions need more than a chart

Marriage decisions involve the wishes and consent of both people, communication, values, expectations, health, finances, family relationships and practical circumstances. These questions are best discussed openly and directly. Astrology may be meaningful as a traditional family practice, but it cannot replace an honest conversation or professional advice where that is needed.

Use private birth information only with the person's permission. Avoid publishing another person's birth details or a report without an appropriate basis to share it.
