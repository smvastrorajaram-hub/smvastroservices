How to use these articles

Read the calculation-method explanation before comparing charts from different websites. For a birth result, check the date, recorded local time, birthplace, coordinates, time-zone handling and ayanamsa. For a traditional interpretation, read the surrounding chart factors rather than relying on a single placement, score or label.

Scope and limitations

Astrological methods are traditional interpretive systems. These guides describe terminology and calculation conventions; they do not claim that astrology scientifically predicts events. They cannot guarantee health, relationship, financial or professional outcomes and should not replace qualified professional advice or personal consent.

Some practices differ by region, lineage and software implementation. Where more than one convention exists, the relevant article identifies that variation instead of presenting one school's rule as universal.
