Who should use astrologer registration?

SMV ASTRO SERVICES provides an online registration route for astrologers who want to submit their details for consideration on the platform. Registration is a request for review; submitting a form does not, by itself, mean an account has been approved or that consultation features are active.

Start with the Register as Astrologer link above and follow the fields displayed in the main registration form. Enter accurate contact details and use an email address and mobile number that you can access when a verification step is required.

Information to prepare

  • Your name and current contact details.
  • A concise description of your astrology experience and the topics you are qualified to discuss.
  • Any professional or training details requested by the actual registration form.
  • Profile details that you are comfortable submitting for the platform's review and any public profile that may be approved.

Provide only relevant information. Never send a password, one-time passcode, payment card PIN or another person's private information as part of a registration enquiry.

Review and approval

After submission, follow the account status and any further instructions shown by SMV ASTRO SERVICES. The platform may review the information before enabling astrologer-specific dashboard functions. Approval and visibility depend on that review and the current platform settings; no approval time or outcome is guaranteed by this information page.

Once your account is approved, sign in through the main website and use only the consultation features actually shown in your account. Keep your profile and contact information current and follow the website's Terms & Conditions and respectful communication rules.

Frequently asked questions

Does registration guarantee that I can answer questions?

No. Registration and approval are separate steps. Use the account status and instructions provided by the platform.

Where do I sign in?

Use the Astrologer Login link to open the login area on the main website.

Who can I contact about a registration issue?

Visit the main website's Contact Us section and include a short description of the problem. Do not include passwords or OTPs.
