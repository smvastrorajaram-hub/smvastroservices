Customer Registration

Find out how to register for SMV ASTRO customer features, access available consultation options and use online horoscope tools.

When should I create a customer account?

A customer account can be used for the account-based features made available by SMV ASTRO SERVICES, including supported question and consultation workflows. You can also visit the public SMV HOROSCOPE tool to see the horoscope features available without starting at the registration page.

To create an account, choose Register as Customer and follow the live registration form. Use your own contact details, complete any verification step requested and keep your login information private.

What to check before registering

  • Use a valid email address and mobile number that you control.
  • Read the privacy notice to understand how account, birth and consultation details are handled.
  • Use a strong, unique password if the registration form asks you to set one; never disclose it to a support contact.
  • Review the service description and any displayed price before placing a paid consultation request.

Using horoscope and matching tools

For a birth chart in Tamil, visit Tamil Horoscope. Enter birth information carefully because a different date, time or birthplace can affect the calculated result. For marriage-related astrological information, visit Marriage Matching. The publicly available matching view may be limited to Nakshatra-based Porutham; additional report sections, when offered, depend on the current access settings.

Account safety and privacy

Share only information needed for the feature you are using. Birth details and consultation questions can be personal. Do not enter passwords, OTPs or bank credentials into a birth-details field, public review or consultation question. Sign out on shared devices and use the contact options on the main website if you believe your account has been accessed incorrectly.

Frequently asked questions

Does creating an account mean every advanced feature is unlocked?

No. The availability of a feature depends on the product rules, your sign-in state and the settings currently shown by the website.

Can I use the horoscope page before registering?

Open SMV HOROSCOPE to see the public tool and the options currently available.

Where can I get help?

Use the main website's Contact Us section and explain the issue without sending secret login codes.
