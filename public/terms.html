5. Billing, cancellation and refunds

Connected live Calls and Chats are settled using the recorded billable duration and the selected Astrologer's applicable per-minute rate. Billing uses whole started minutes: a connected duration greater than zero counts as at least one billable minute, and each additional started minute is billed at the displayed rate. Eligible Bonus Free Minutes are applied first, with the paid Wallet balance used for the remaining charge.

When a live interaction does not connect, or a scheduled booking is cancelled, rejected or expires before service, the reserved amount is released or reconciled according to the final session or booking status. A reservation release back to Wallet is different from a refund to the original payment method. If a refund is applicable and approved, it is processed through the relevant payment route where supported; provider and bank processing times may differ.

For a missing Wallet credit, duplicate charge, incorrect charge or unresolved session settlement, contact us with the session / booking ID and payment reference. We will review the transaction and status. Disagreement with an Astrologer's opinion does not by itself mean that a consultation was not delivered. Nothing in this section limits an applicable statutory refund or consumer remedy.
