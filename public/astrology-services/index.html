Explore SMV ASTRO services

SMV ASTRO SERVICES brings together online horoscope calculations, Tamil horoscope tools, traditional marriage matching information and ways to contact an astrologer. This page explains what each service is for and helps you choose the appropriate next step.

  • Online horoscope: enter a birth date, birth time and birthplace to create a chart and view the results available in the SMV HOROSCOPE tool.
  • Tamil Jathagam: switch to Tamil to read supported horoscope information in Tamil, or use English where available.
  • Marriage matching: open the separate matching tool to view the publicly available Nakshatra-based Porutham results and any additional analysis enabled for your access.
  • Panchang and calendar: visit SMV CALENDAR for the date-based calendar and traditional Panchang features provided there.
  • Astrologer consultation: review the available consultation options if you need a personal discussion rather than an automated chart.

How to use the online horoscope

Use the date and time recorded for the birth, and select the closest accurate birthplace. An incorrect birth time or place may change the computed chart. After entering details, follow the controls shown by the tool and review the report headings to understand which calculations are included.

Some advanced features may depend on sign-in or the current access settings. Read the on-screen instructions before assuming that every report section is available in every session. Do not submit passwords, one-time passcodes or payment credentials in a horoscope field.

Choosing a service

For a birth chart in Tamil, start at Tamil Horoscope. For traditional compatibility information, open Marriage Matching. To create an account for supported customer features, review Customer Registration. Astrologers who wish to submit details for platform review can read Astrologer Registration.

Important note about astrology

Horoscope calculations and interpretations reflect a traditional astrological framework. They are not guarantees of future events and should not replace qualified medical, legal, financial or other professional advice. Use the information as cultural or personal guidance and make important decisions using the full circumstances and appropriate professional help.

Frequently asked questions

Can I open the horoscope tool directly?

Yes. Use the SMV HOROSCOPE link above. The tool will show the fields and features available for the current session.

Does marriage matching give a guaranteed decision?

No. An astrological compatibility report is one traditional perspective, not a guarantee of relationship success or a substitute for the individuals’ consent, conversations and decisions.
