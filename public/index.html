FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

FAQ

How do I ask an astrology question?

Login as a customer, choose Ask Your Questions, enter the birth details and question, then complete the secure payment flow.

Are astrologers verified?

All astrologers are carefully reviewed and verified by SMV ASTRO before being approved for consultation services.

How can I contact SMV ASTRO?

Use the Contact Us form to send your query directly.

Do I need an account?

An account is required for secure customer consultation features. You can create one from Customer Registration or the Login button.

How are astrologers selected and verified?

All astrologers are carefully reviewed and verified by SMV ASTRO before being approved for consultation services.

When will I receive my astrology answer?

After successful payment and submission, your astrology question will normally be answered within 24 hours.

Is there a refund or cancellation policy?

Payments are generally non-refundable once an accepted question enters the consultation process. If SMV ASTRO cannot provide the purchased service, a refund may be considered.

What happens if my question is not suitable for the astrology service?

Questions that are unrelated to astrology, inappropriate, abusive, or involve matters that SMV ASTRO cannot responsibly provide guidance on may be rejected. If the question is rejected before consultation is provided, the customer may be eligible for a refund, normally processed within 48 hours of the rejection decision.

Are astrology answers guaranteed?

No. Astrology is a traditional form of guidance and interpretation. It is not a guarantee of future events or outcomes. Customers are free to accept, consider, or disagree with the guidance provided.

How do offers and promotion codes work?

Eligible offers may be applied automatically at payment, or you can enter a valid promotion code when the option is available. Only one eligible offer is applied to a payment, and the final amount is verified securely before payment.

How does the ₹1 New Customer Welcome Offer work?

When the ₹1 Welcome Offer is enabled, an eligible new customer can use it for the applicable first successful question. A failed or cancelled payment does not count as a successful use of the offer.

Are festival and seasonal offers available?

SMV ASTRO may enable limited-time festival or seasonal promotions. Availability, eligibility, price and promotion-code requirements depend on the active offer at the time of payment.

How does astrologer qualification and auto approval work?

When the qualification system is enabled, a registered astrologer may be required to complete the SMV ASTRO Google Form qualification test. If the verified score reaches the required pass mark, the account can be approved automatically. When auto approval is disabled, the existing Admin review and approval process continues.
