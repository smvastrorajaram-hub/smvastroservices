ABOUT SMV ASTRO

Sri Madurai Veerayah Astro Services

SMV ASTRO brings traditional Vedic astrology into an accessible online experience. Explore your horoscope using your birth details, learn through our astrology articles and connect with approved astrologers for personal consultations.

Our service combines respect for tradition with clear, thoughtful guidance on the questions that matter to you.

Non-AI calculation & prediction method: SMV ASTRO Horoscope, Marriage Matching, Panchang, Transit, Calendar and automated prediction outputs are produced by programmed astronomical/Vedic-astrology calculation and rule engines. They are not AI-generated and not AI-calculated, and the audited calculation flow does not send birth/chart data to a generative-AI model. Personal consultation answers are handled separately by approved astrologers.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

FAQ

How do I ask an astrology question?

Login as a customer, choose Ask Your Questions, enter the birth details and question, then complete the secure payment flow.

Are astrologers verified?

All astrologers are carefully reviewed and verified by SMV ASTRO before being approved for consultation services.

How can I contact SMV ASTRO?

Use the Contact Us form to send your query directly.

Do I need an account?

An account is required for secure customer consultation features. You can create one from Customer Registration or the Login button.

When will I receive my astrology answer?

After successful payment and submission, your astrology question will normally be answered within 24 hours.

Is there a refund or cancellation policy?

Payments are generally non-refundable once an accepted question enters the consultation process. If SMV ASTRO cannot provide the purchased service, a refund may be considered.

What happens if my question is not suitable for the astrology service?

Questions that are unrelated to astrology, inappropriate, abusive, or involve matters that SMV ASTRO cannot responsibly provide guidance on may be rejected. If the question is rejected before consultation is provided, the customer may be eligible for a refund, normally processed within 48 hours of the rejection decision.

Are astrology answers guaranteed?

No. Astrology is a traditional form of guidance and interpretation. It is not a guarantee of future events or outcomes. Customers are free to accept, consider, or disagree with the guidance provided.

How do offers and promotion codes work?

Eligible offers may be applied automatically at payment, or you can enter a valid promotion code when the option is available. Only one eligible offer is applied to a payment, and the final amount is verified securely before payment.

How does the ₹1 New Customer Welcome Offer work?

When the ₹1 Welcome Offer is enabled, an eligible new customer can use it for the applicable first successful question. A failed or cancelled payment does not count as a successful use of the offer.

Are festival and seasonal offers available?

SMV ASTRO may enable limited-time festival or seasonal promotions. Availability, eligibility, price and promotion-code requirements depend on the active offer at the time of payment.

How does astrologer qualification and auto approval work?

When the qualification system is enabled, a registered astrologer may be required to complete the SMV ASTRO Google Form qualification test. If the verified score reaches the required pass mark, the account can be approved automatically. When auto approval is disabled, the existing Admin review and approval process continues.

Does SMV ASTRO use AI to calculate or generate astrology results?

No. Horoscope, Marriage Matching, Panchang, Transit, SMV CALENDAR and automated prediction sections are produced by programmed astronomical/Vedic-astrology calculation and rule engines. They are not AI-generated and not AI-calculated. Personal consultation answers are a separate human astrologer service.

What does SMV CALENDAR provide?

SMV CALENDAR provides date-based Panchang, Hora, planetary transit chart and positions, Moon-sign transit readings, Chandrashtama indication, Tara Bala, Tamil-calendar information, listed vrats, festivals and supported holiday references.

Can I use SMV CALENDAR in Tamil and English?

Yes. SMV CALENDAR supports Tamil and English display. The language switch reuses the calculated calendar data for display instead of intentionally repeating the full ephemeris calculation just to change language.

How does place search work in Horoscope and SMV CALENDAR?

Supported Indian place searches use online place lookup when a connection is available and can fall back to bundled offline India place data. Latitude and longitude can also be entered manually when needed.

What can I see before full Horoscope or Marriage Matching access is available?

The site can show the basic horoscope and, when full marriage matching is not open, the Nakshatra Porutham layer. Full Horoscope and Marriage Matching availability is controlled by the active SMV ASTRO feature settings.

Can full Horoscope or Marriage Matching require payment?

Yes. When the relevant feature is enabled with a configured price, the full-report index can be shown first and access is unlocked after the required customer login and verified payment. If the configured price is zero, the enabled feature can be available without a report payment.

How are saved Horoscope and Marriage Matching reports handled?

For signed-in customers, eligible full reports can be saved in the browser and listed under Saved Reports. Saved reports can be reopened, deleted, or sent to the browser print flow for printing or Save as PDF.

Does SMV ASTRO support offline use?

SMV HOROSCOPE and SMV CALENDAR include installable web-app support and cache supported calculation assets and bundled India place-search data for offline fallback. Initial installation and some account, payment or online-search functions still require an internet connection.

Are automated predictions the same as a personal astrologer consultation?

No. Automated horoscope prediction sections follow programmed chart rules and calculated planetary data. A personal consultation is a separate service in which an approved astrologer reviews the customer’s question and provides an individual answer.

What does “non-AI calculation and prediction” mean on SMV ASTRO?

It means the astrology result is produced from entered birth or matching data by programmed astronomical and Vedic-astrology rules. The system does not ask a generative-AI model to calculate the chart, invent a prediction, rewrite the result, or decide the matching verdict.

Are my birth details or horoscope data sent to ChatGPT, OpenAI, Gemini or another generative-AI service?

No in the audited SMV ASTRO source. Horoscope, Marriage Matching, Panchang, Transit and automated prediction calculations do not use a generative-AI API. Online services can still be used for account authentication, payments, email, hosting or place lookup where the relevant feature requires them.

What affects the accuracy of an automated horoscope result?

The result depends on the birth date, exact birth time, selected birthplace or coordinates and the calculation rules used by SMV ASTRO. An incorrect or approximate birth time or place can change Lagna, house positions, divisional charts, Dasa timing and therefore rule-based interpretations.

Does changing Tamil / English create a new prediction?

No. Where language switching is supported, SMV ASTRO is designed to reuse the same calculated astrology data and present it in the selected language instead of asking an AI model to regenerate the prediction.

Which parts still need an internet connection?

Account login, Firestore-backed dashboard data, consultation workflows, payment verification, email delivery and online place lookup require their respective online services. Supported Horoscope and SMV CALENDAR calculation assets and bundled India place data can work from the installed/cached web app when available offline.

Are Saved Reports the same as consultation records?

No. Eligible Horoscope and Marriage Matching Saved Reports are browser-side report records for reopening or printing on that browser profile. Customer consultation questions, answers, payment status and account records are separate service data associated with the signed-in account.

Can I rely on an automated prediction for medical, legal or financial decisions?

No. Astrology is traditional guidance and does not replace qualified medical, legal, financial or other professional advice. Automated predictions and astrologer consultations do not guarantee a future event or outcome.
