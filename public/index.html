FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

FAQ

How do I start a consultation?

Sign in as a Customer, open Consult an Astrologer, and review the Astrologer List. Each profile may show languages, availability, Call and Chat rates per minute, and published reviews. Choose a live Call or Chat when available, or request a listed appointment time through Book a Call or Book a Chat.

What is the difference between Live Call / Chat and a booking?

A live request is for a consultation now and proceeds when the selected Astrologer accepts and the connection is established. A booking requests a scheduled Call or Chat; check its status and acceptance in your Dashboard. Wallet funds may be reserved while a booking is pending and are released or settled according to its final status.

How are live consultation charges calculated?

Each Astrologer can have separate per-minute Call and Chat rates. Review the applicable rate before starting or booking. A connected session is billed in started whole minutes: any connected duration greater than zero counts as at least one billable minute, with additional started minutes billed at the displayed rate. Eligible bonus free minutes are applied before paid Wallet balance.

How do I add money to My Wallet?

Sign in as a Customer, open Dashboard → My Wallet, select Recharge Wallet, and complete Razorpay checkout. Wallet credit is added only after successful payment is verified. You can review recharge and transaction records in the Wallet. If a payment remains pending or money was debited without matching Wallet credit, contact support with the payment reference before making a duplicate payment.

How do Wallet Recharge Bonus Free Minutes work?

Only active offers published by SMV ASTRO apply. An offer can specify a minimum recharge, validity period, and per-customer or overall usage limits. At most one matching offer applies to a recharge. Bonus minutes are promotional, have no cash value, and cannot be withdrawn or transferred. For eligible live Call and Chat sessions, bonus minutes are used before paid Wallet balance.

What happens if a live session does not connect or a booking is cancelled?

The recorded session or booking status determines settlement. Funds reserved for a session that does not connect, or for a booking cancelled, rejected, or expired before service, are released or reconciled according to that final status. If your Wallet history does not reflect a completed settlement, contact support with the session or booking ID and transaction reference. Applicable legal rights are not limited by this explanation.

Where can I see bookings and transactions?

Open your signed-in Dashboard. My Wallet shows the available balance, recharge history, and Wallet transactions. The relevant booking or session view shows Call / Chat status. Keep transaction and booking references when contacting support.

Can customers withdraw Wallet funds?

The Customer Wallet is designed for eligible SMV ASTRO service payments and does not provide a general cash-withdrawal feature. It is separate from the earnings and payout process available to approved Astrologers. Any applicable release or refund is handled under the transaction status, refund process, and applicable law.

How are Astrologers approved, and can I see reviews?

Registration alone does not activate consultation access. Astrologers must be approved under the current SMV ASTRO review or qualification settings. Profiles display the details made available for publication, and approved customer reviews may appear on an Astrologer profile.

Does SMV ASTRO guarantee an astrology outcome?

No. Astrology and Astrologer guidance are traditional interpretations, not guarantees of future events or results. They are not a substitute for qualified medical, legal, financial, or other professional advice.

Does a live consultation use an AI bot?

No. Live voice Call and live Chat are intended to connect you with the selected approved Astrologer. This live consultation feature is distinct from automated astrology tools linked elsewhere on the website.

Where can I browse SMV ASTRO products or get help?

The Products section links to the external SMV ASTRO Shoopy store, where current product prices and availability are shown. For account, Wallet, booking, or privacy enquiries, use the Contact Us form or email smvastroservices@gmail.com. External providers apply their own terms to services you use on their sites.
