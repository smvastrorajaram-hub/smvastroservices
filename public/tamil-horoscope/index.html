A Tamil horoscope can help readers explore the chart information using familiar Tamil terms alongside English labels where available. The exact results depend on the birth details entered and the calculations and report sections currently provided by the tool.

Details to enter carefully

  • Birth date: check the day, month and year.
  • Birth time: enter the recorded local time as accurately as possible. An approximate time can affect time-sensitive chart factors.
  • Birthplace: select the correct town or place rather than a different city with a similar name.
  • Language: use the Tamil/English control provided by SMV HOROSCOPE to read the available results in your preferred language.

Understanding common horoscope sections

Depending on the available report, a horoscope may show Rasi and planetary placements, houses, Nakshatra information, divisional charts or Dasa periods. Read each heading and its accompanying notes instead of treating one table or one placement as a complete interpretation. If an item is not shown in your current view, do not assume it was calculated or included.

For traditional marriage compatibility information, open Marriage Matching / ஜாதகப் பொருத்தம். The public matching view may begin with Nakshatra-based Porutham; additional analysis depends on the current access settings.

Frequently asked questions

Will an approximate birth time give exactly the same chart?

Not necessarily. Some time-sensitive chart factors may change when the entered time changes. Use the most reliable birth-time record available.

Are horoscope results guaranteed predictions?

No. They are traditional astrological calculations and interpretations, not guarantees of an event or substitute for qualified professional advice.
