What is Jathaga Porutham?

Jathaga Porutham is a traditional astrological approach used by some families when discussing marriage compatibility. The SMV ASTRO matching tool helps users review the match information made available by the site. The report should be read as one cultural and interpretive perspective, not as a guaranteed decision about a relationship.

The public matching view may start with Nakshatra-based Porutham. More extensive analysis, where it is enabled and available, can depend on current access settings and the report controls displayed by the tool.

Information to prepare

Use accurate details for both people. Depending on the form, you may be asked for each person's birth date, recorded birth time and birthplace. If a birth time is unknown, do not invent an exact time; note the limitation when interpreting time-sensitive parts of any report.

  • Check each person's date separately before submitting.
  • Choose the correct birthplace for each chart.
  • Make sure you know which person's details are in each form section.
  • Use another person's personal birth details only when you have an appropriate reason and permission.

How to read the matching report

Start with the result summary, then read the specific factors and explanations that are actually shown. A single score or factor does not describe the whole relationship. Traditional methods may assess different considerations and interpret them differently; avoid treating an automated label as a final verdict about two people.

Compatibility conversations, mutual consent, communication, values, expectations and practical circumstances remain important. An astrology report cannot guarantee relationship success, resolve a disagreement by itself, or replace the decisions of the people involved.
