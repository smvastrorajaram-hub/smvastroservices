# SMV ASTRO Muhurta source audit (1800–2399)

Status: research dossier for the independent Tamil-traditional and Pan-Indian engines. No production deployment is performed by this work.

## Evidence policy

A named book or a modern annual calendar is not, by itself, proof of a timeless rule. Each engine rule must carry a stable rule ID and activity ID; kind (REQUIRED, PREFERRED, PROHIBITED, CONDITIONAL); exact source work, edition, chapter/verse or printed page; exceptions and region/lineage scope; and review state.

Uncollated, ambiguous, or secondary-only rules remain pending and cannot cause a pass/fail result. Personal horoscope matching is separate from electional time and requires explicit user data. No rule based on caste or sex is implemented.

## Tamil-traditional primary and supporting references

1. **Vidhānamālai (விதானமாலை)** — Tamil text, Ripon Press, Madras, 1900 print; catalogue identifies an 1881 first edition, 210 pages. Primary Tamil verse source. Relevant divisions:
   - Chapter 2, Pañcāṅkam: tithi preferences for marriage, Vāstu, travel, vehicles and house-entry.
   - Chapter 3, Guṇaguṇam: weekday/nakshatra/tithi yogas and exceptions.
   - Chapter 11, Kaṭimaṇam: marriage compatibility and marriage election; compatibility (dina, gaṇa, yoni, rāśi, rajju, etc.) is not conflated with date/time evaluation.
   - Chapter 16, Maintharvinai: child rites, annaprāśana, education and upanayana.
   - Chapter 25, Echchavinai: activity-specific lagna/house-purity references for marriage, travel, feeding, Vāstu and house-entry.
   Digital text: https://jyotisha.vedicreader.com/ (1900 Ripon Press print transcript; chapter deep links are in the code source registry). Catalogue: https://tamildigitallibrary.in/

2. **Kālaprakāśikā (காலபிரகாசிகை)** — Tamil Digital Library catalogue: Sarasvati Mahal Library, 2014, 7th edition, bilingual, 248 pages. The accessible full text used during this audit was an English translation/reprint attributed to N. P. Subramania Iyer (1917 first publication; later AES reprint), not the 2014 Tamil pages. Chapters include naming, feeding, ear-piercing, tonsure, education, upanayana, marriage, pumsavana/seemantani, agriculture, foundation, opening ceremony, deity installation and travel. It is a corroborative South-Indian reference only until the Tamil edition is collated. English text: https://www.astrojyoti.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/Kalaprakasika_text.pdf

3. **TTD Subhakṛtu Tamil Panchangam 2022–2023** — contemporary published Panchang example using Lahiri/Thirukanitha calculation. It lists many auspicious activities and provides separate sections for activities, house-entry, travel and agriculture. It is a year-specific publication and is not treated as a universal 600-year rulebook. Use only as an editorial cross-check.

4. **Kālavidhānam (காலவிதானம்)** — named in an astrology syllabus bibliography and cited by later Panchang practice, but an independently inspectable primary edition was not obtained during this pass. All Kālavidhānam-derived rules remain pending until a specific edition/page is checked.

5. **KAHE M.Sc. Applied Astrology syllabus (2019–2020)** — secondary scope/bibliography evidence only; lists sixteen saṃskāras, marriage, travel, grahapraveśa and common muhurta yogas, and references Kālaprakāśikā and Kālavidhānam. It is not itself a rule authority.

## Pan-Indian primary and supporting references

1. **Muhūrta Cintāmaṇi (मुहूर्तचिन्तामणिः), Rāma Daivajña** — primary Sanskrit electional treatise. The publicly accessible Maharishi University of Management Vedic Literature Collection PDF contains the Sanskrit text. Chapter map:
   - 1 Śubhāśubha: general weekday/tithi/nakshatra combinations and defects (including activity-specific exceptions at 1.22).
   - 2 Graha-Nakṣatra: star classes and activity suitability.
   - 5 Saṃskāra: pregnancy/child rites and educational rites.
   - 6 Vivāha: marriage election; includes separate marriage and personal-chart conditions.
   - 10 Rājābhiṣeka.
   - 11 Yātrā: journey election.
   - 12 Vāstu: site/building and layout (not every verse is a date/time election).
   - 13 Gṛhapraveśa: house-entry election.
   Accessible text: https://vedicreserve.miu.edu/jyotish/muhurta_cintamani.pdf

2. **Printed-edition check** — Narayanram Acharya, Nirnaya Sagar Press, 1945, Sanskrit/Hindi, 484 pages; one scanned printed page of the Vivāha chapter was separately located. IGNCA’s Sanskrit manuscript catalogue records multiple complete/incomplete Muhūrta Cintāmaṇi manuscripts attributed to Rāma Daivajña. Edition/verse numbering must be recorded per source edition.

3. **Muhūrta Mārtaṇḍa, Nārāyaṇa Daivajña** — separate classical corroborating text with saṃskāra, marriage, building/house, and travel chapters. An 1819 DLI scan and later Kashi Sanskrit Series catalogue record were identified; exact rule-by-rule comparison is pending. It must not silently override Muhūrta Cintāmaṇi.

4. **Nirṇaya Sindhu, Kamalākara Bhaṭṭa** — supporting Dharma-nibandha for ritual and calendar observance (tithi/sankranti/vrata rules), not a universal substitute for the muhurta manuals. An 1888/1901 printed tradition is catalogued; only specific scanned page rules may be promoted into the engine.

5. **Dharmasindhu** — additional ritual-calendar cross-reference; not directly implemented as a muhurta election source in this pass.

## Implementation boundary

The new engines accept a normalized, neutral astronomical snapshot produced from existing SMV Swiss Ephemeris + Thirukanitha Panchanga calculations. They do not recalculate ephemerides, and they do not share tradition-specific rules, scoring, exceptions or evaluator logic. Current global online GeoNames/timezone resolution remains untouched; no global offline location/timezone dataset is added.

The current annual list (2026/2027) is retained as a legacy publication-specific feature until a separate UI migration is verified. It is not used as a 1800–2399 computational rule engine.
